# Visual system

## Default brand preset

- Format: 3:4 portrait, target 1080×1440 or higher equivalent.
- Background: warm off-white with generous editorial whitespace.
- Palette: cobalt blue, coral orange, warm yellow, magenta pink, teal green.
- Material: rounded Cinema 4D forms covered in dense, even, very-short microfiber plush.
- Lighting: soft diffused studio light, subtle colored glow, gentle contact shadows.
- Tone: tactile, playful, premium, beginner-friendly technology editorial.
- Avoid: dark cyberpunk, generic robot heads, dense dashboards, long shaggy fur, photoreal people, excessive decoration.

## Typography

- Use dimensional short-fur lettering with clean edges.
- Keep the cover keyword largest and centered.
- Keep “什么是” or an equivalent setup line substantially smaller than the keyword.
- Keep explanatory copy brief. If furry type reduces readability, reserve it for headlines and render supporting copy in a crisp matching typeface.
- Never accept malformed Chinese. Regenerate or add type deterministically.

## Fixed corners

Default labels:

- top left: `2026`
- top right: `GuangYing`
- bottom left: `AI`
- bottom right: `{{关键词}}`

Keep all four small, consistent, inside safe margins, and visually secondary. Treat one English technical term such as `Agent` or `AIGC` as one keyword even if it exceeds four Latin characters. Keep Chinese keywords to four characters or fewer when possible.

## Interior-page composition

- Top: question, distinction, or page takeaway.
- Middle: one dominant visual metaphor or continuous story.
- Bottom: labels, sequence, or closing sentence.
- Do not center explanatory paragraphs in the middle.
- Characters are optional. When present, use faceless minimal figures unless identity matters.
- Objects and decorative elements must explain the content.

## Consistency locks

After the cover is approved, lock:

1. background temperature;
2. five-color palette;
3. fur length and density;
4. light direction and shadow softness;
5. headline scale;
6. corner positions and sizes;
7. character construction;
8. edge-glow intensity.

Use the approved cover and latest approved interior page as references for subsequent generations.
