# Publish-ready checklist

## Content

- [ ] The post delivers one clear promise for an AI-curious beginner.
- [ ] Claims were checked against credible current sources.
- [ ] Facts, viewpoints, analogies, and inference are not blurred together.
- [ ] Each page has one job and one memorable takeaway.
- [ ] Examples match the mechanism being explained.
- [ ] Capability claims include necessary limits or approvals.

## Copy

- [ ] One recommended title and two alternatives are present.
- [ ] The body is under 200 Chinese characters unless overridden.
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant and not stuffed.
- [ ] Every Chinese character and English brand label is correct.
- [ ] No generated placeholder, gibberish, watermark, or unintended logo remains.

## Visuals

- [ ] At least four images are present and ordered.
- [ ] No more than eight images are present unless the longer set was explicitly approved.
- [ ] Every image is 3:4 portrait at sufficient resolution.
- [ ] The cover title is dominant and mobile-readable.
- [ ] Explanatory text stays at the top or bottom.
- [ ] The center is image-led.
- [ ] Palette, fur density, lighting, corners, character style, and margins are consistent.
- [ ] No page imitates a reference artwork too closely.
- [ ] Every central object supports the page's educational job.

## Files

- [ ] Files use zero-padded publishing order, such as `01-cover.png`.
- [ ] Final assets are stored in the project, not only in a temporary generation folder.
- [ ] `validate_carousel.py` exits successfully.
